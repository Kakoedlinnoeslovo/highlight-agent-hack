About this scouting sample

One short visit per corner, recorded at different times of day. Audience counts are vision-model estimates from one frame per five seconds, not unique people. Premium-car and people-type calls are judgements from single frames. Check the evidence before deciding; a lease decision needs longer, comparable recordings plus rent and competition.

The business profile is generated when you search. Rankings use published, cached frame observations, and the evidence videos are pre-cut from the recorded sample. These are not live street measurements.
